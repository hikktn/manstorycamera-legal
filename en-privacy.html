ManStoryCamera Privacy Policy

Scope and contact

This policy covers the local creation features of ManStoryCamera 1.0. The operator is 李松林. Contact: lilin1213457107@gmail.com. Policy version: local-v1-2026-10-31-r1.

Media and creation on your device

Photos, Live Photos, short videos, existing media metadata, drawings, references, theme preferences and projects are processed on your device for creation, preview and saving. On-device visual analysis helps composition and subject protection, not identification of your real identity. This version does not offer cloud redrawing, account registration, membership purchases or automatic cloud sync. It does not automatically send media or usage events to the operator.

System permissions

Taking photos requires camera access. Recording video with sound requires microphone access. Selection and saving use the system photo features when requested. You may refuse or revoke permissions in system settings; the related feature may then be unavailable. Local photo composition does not require location or speech recognition permission. The system keyboard, Photos, iCloud backups and sharing destinations operate under their own services and your settings.

Storage and deletion

Media, projects and preferences are stored on your device and managed through the app and system. Deleting a project does not automatically erase copies in Photos, shared copies or system backups. This version adds no media, account or automatic event records on the operator's server. Upgrading does not erase account, feedback or transaction records sent by older versions. Contact us to ask about or request handling of older records; we must verify the relevant information first.

Support and sharing you initiate

If you choose to email support, the operator receives the contact details, message and attachments you include to handle your request. Your chosen email service transmits the message. Include only what is needed to resolve the issue, without passwords or unnecessary private media. You initiate sharing or saving to other services; their own rules apply.

Your choices and updates

You can manage permissions, delete local projects, stop using the app, or email requests for access, correction, deletion and privacy assistance. An older self-test agreement is not treated as this public policy. Material changes will be presented with a new version, and new network processing will be explained before it is offered. Guardians should guide minors and avoid exposing children's private information in projects or support messages.
